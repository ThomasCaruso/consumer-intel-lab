Consumer Intelligence Lab

Allow your dashboard to read your Chrome history?

Chrome will ask you to confirm. You can switch this off at any time.

What is read

  • Page addresses (URLs) and page titles you visited
  • When each visit happened
  • Only for the period you pick on the dashboard (max 365 days)

What is never read

  • Passwords, cookies, or sign-in tokens
  • Page contents, form entries, or downloads
  • Login/session parameters (stripped from URLs)
History is handed only to your own dashboard page and analyzed inside your browser. It is not uploaded to any server, AI service, or analytics tool, and it is not saved after you close the tab.
